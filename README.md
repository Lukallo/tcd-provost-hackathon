## Judging, at a glance

| Criterion | Weight | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| Potential for Impact | 25% | Name a number: people reached, kg diverted, € saved, CO₂ avoided |
| Challenge Alignment | 20% | Say which SDG targets, out loud, in the pitch |
| Innovative Approach | 20% | Know the existing solutions and say why ours is different |
| Feasibility | 20% | Show a first step that could run in Trinity next term |
| Human-Centred Design | 15% | Quote real people we spoke to; show the prototype was tested |

## Deliverables on the day

1. A clearly defined sustainability problem
2. A proposed solution
3. A simple prototype (mock-up, storyboard, service blueprint, model, campaign…)
4. Key assumptions to explore further
5. A three-minute pitch
