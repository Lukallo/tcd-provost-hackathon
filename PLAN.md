## Templates

### Problem statement
> **[Who]** struggles to **[do what]** because **[root cause]**, which leads to **[social/environmental cost]**.
> We know this because **[evidence: interviews + numbers]**.

### Value proposition
> For **[user]** who **[need]**, **[our solution]** is a **[service/product/campaign]** that **[key benefit]**.
> Unlike **[existing option]**, we **[difference]**.

### Key assumptions (fill in on the day; this is a required deliverable)

| Assumption | Type | How we'd test it | Risk if wrong |
|---|---|---|---|
| e.g. Students will donate kit at move-out | Desirability | Pilot one hall, count donations | High |
| e.g. Summer storage costs < €X | Feasibility | Ask Accommodation Office | Medium |
| e.g. Each kit saves a new student €Y | Impact | Price the kit vs. new | Medium |

### 3-minute pitch skeleton
1. **Hook (15s)** — one person, one moment. A quote from an interview works best.
2. **Problem (30s)** — scale with a sourced number; which SDG.
3. **Solution (45s)** — what it is, shown through the prototype.
4. **Why us / why different (20s)** — the incumbent and the gap.
5. **Impact (30s)** — year-one number for Trinity; what scaling looks like.
6. **Feasibility (25s)** — first step next term, who runs it, rough cost.
7. **Ask / close (15s)** — what you need next (Hub support, a pilot site, a partner).
